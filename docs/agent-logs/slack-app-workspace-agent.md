## Key finding (launch-critical)
Since May 29, 2025, new installs of commercially distributed apps that are not Marketplace-approved get
conversations.history and conversations.replies at 1 req/min with 15 objects per call (Tier 1). Marketplace
and internal apps keep Tier 3. Existing installs are grandfathered.
